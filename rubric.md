# Rubric for the AI-scored items (G1-G4, S1-S2)

Scored by Claude (an AI) reading each Profile A answer (thinking off). This is a rough signal, not a formal evaluation.
Maximum per model: 6 items x 2 points = 12.

## General explanation questions (G1-G4)
- 2: factually correct, answers the question that was asked, clear, respects any requested length or audience.
  G1 must name scattering of shorter (blue) wavelengths by air molecules (Rayleigh scattering).
  G2 must say RAM is fast, temporary/volatile working memory and storage is slower, persistent.
  G3 must define inflation as a general rise in prices / fall in purchasing power and give two valid causes (e.g. demand-pull, cost-push, money supply growth, expectations).
  G4 must name axial tilt (about 23.4 degrees) as the cause and explicitly correct the distance misconception.
- 1: mostly right but with a notable error, a missing required element, or ignores the requested format.
- 0: wrong, off-topic, empty, or mainly hallucinated.

## Summaries of the fixed 295-word passage (S1, S2)
- 2: faithful (no invented facts or numbers), captures the core: library lends free seeds, started 2019 by a volunteer, grew (about 120 to 600+ households / 90+ varieties), some problems, benefits. S2 may compress to the main idea only.
- 1: mostly faithful but misses the main point or contains one factual error.
- 0: unfaithful, invents facts, or is not a summary.
The word limit is checked separately and automatically (S1 at most 60 words, S2 under 20 words).
